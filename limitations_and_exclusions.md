# Limitations and Exclusions
## Online Warehouse Rental Management System (OWRMS)

> [!NOTE]
> This document defines the boundaries of the OWRMS platform scope based on a review of the implemented domain model, application features, and system architecture. All items below reflect deliberate design decisions, not implementation gaps.

---

## A. Limitations

The following limitations describe capabilities that are partially implemented or architecturally constrained by the current system design.

**LI-01 – Contract Signing Legality**
The system supports a full digital contract lifecycle including drafting, owner and renter signature capture, activation, extension, termination, and two-party closure approval. However, contracts generated by the platform do not carry legal enforceability under Vietnamese e-signature law (Nghị định 130/2018/NĐ-CP), as the platform does not integrate a certified digital signature authority (CA). Signatures are stored as Base64 image captures and serve as intent records only.

**LI-02 – Identity Verification**
User registration is validated by email and password only. The system does not perform eKYC identity verification, business license validation, or tax code verification for Owner accounts. Warehouse listings are reviewed by an administrator before approval, but no legal entity verification is applied.

**LI-03 – Web-Only Platform**
The system is delivered exclusively as a web-based application (React SPA + ASP.NET Core REST API). Native mobile applications for Android or iOS are not included in scope. The interface is responsive but not optimized for mobile-native interactions.

**LI-04 – Physical Safety and Surveillance**
The platform manages equipment records, tracks incidents, and logs maintenance cycles. It does not integrate with physical surveillance infrastructure, IoT camera networks, or AI-based fire detection and safety monitoring systems. The `IotDeviceId` field on the [Equipment](file:///d:/doan5/OnlineWarehouseRentelManagementSystem/backend/WMS.Domain/Entities/Equipment.cs#6-50) entity is a placeholder only and is not connected to any external IoT service.

**LI-05 – Rating System Constraints**
A warehouse rating and review mechanism is implemented, allowing renters to submit star scores, comments, and owners to reply. However, ratings are not currently factored into warehouse search rankings or listing scores. There is also no enforcement that a rating must be submitted only after a confirmed completed rental.

**LI-06 – Listing Content Integrity**
Warehouse listings and price updates submitted by owners are subject to administrator approval. The system does not apply any automated mechanism to detect false, misleading, or inaccurate listing content. Accuracy of listing information depends entirely on manual administrator review.

**LI-07 – Payment Gateway Coverage**
Rental payments are processed through SePay bank transfer integration, with webhook-based auto-confirmation and a cash payment fallback confirmed by the owner. Other domestic gateways (VNPay, MoMo, ZaloPay) and international card payments are not supported.

**LI-08 – Notification Delivery**
The system maintains an internal notification store for in-app alerts. Email or SMS push notifications for critical events such as contract expiry, payment overdue, or signature deadline are not integrated.

**LI-09 – Staff Management Scope**
Warehouse staff are managed via a membership model with role-based access (Checker, Inventory Operator, Warehouse Worker) scoped to individual warehouses. There is no cross-warehouse staff sharing or centralized workforce management capability.

## B. Exclusions

The following items are entirely outside the system's operational scope, by design.

**EX-01 – Physical Warehouse Operations**
The system does not participate in the physical operation of the warehouse. Inbound and outbound requests are submitted and approved through the platform, and tasks are assigned to staff to mirror operational status. The actual physical handling of goods — counting, stacking, moving, or staging — is carried out entirely outside the system.

**EX-02 – Legal Liability for Disputes**
The platform functions strictly as an information intermediary connecting lessees and lessors. It bears no legal responsibility for contractual disputes, failed rental arrangements, property damage claims, or breaches of agreement arising between the two parties.

**EX-03 – Asset and Infrastructure Inspection**
The system provides a post-contract return inspection checklist (covering cleanliness, equipment integrity, and outstanding debts) and allows inspection findings to be recorded. It does not provide professional third-party warehouse quality inspection services or perform legal verification of physical assets or infrastructure.

**EX-04 – Auction and Bidding Mechanisms**
The platform does not support auction-based or competitive bidding mechanisms for warehouse rentals. Pricing is determined by the warehouse owner, and no real-time bid-based allocation is implemented.

**EX-05 – Price and Contract Term Negotiation**
The system allows owners to propose a monthly payment when generating a contract from an approved rental request. It does not provide a built-in negotiation channel, counter-offer flow, or structured communication thread for discussing contract terms. All negotiation takes place outside the platform.

**EX-06 – Fraud Prevention Liability**
The system does not perform fraud screening on user-submitted information, listing content, or financial claims. The platform is not responsible for fraudulent activities, identity deception, or false information submitted by users on either side of a rental arrangement.

**EX-07 – Warehouse Personnel Services**
The platform provides tools for warehouse owners to manage their own staff, assign shifts, and track task completion. It does not act as an HR provider, staffing agency, or on-site security service. All employment and operational personnel decisions remain the sole responsibility of the warehouse owner.

**EX-08 – Inventory Ownership and Liability**
The system tracks renter inventory at the item level within a contracted warehouse. The platform is not responsible for the safety, insurance, loss, or damage of goods stored in the warehouse. Physical responsibility for renter assets lies entirely with the contracting parties.
